Quick answers to the most frequent passenger inquiries.

Cityline connects directly to live ADS-B satellite radar feeds to continuously monitor your commercial flight or private charter. Chauffeur dispatch times are automatically calibrated based on actual touchdown timestamps rather than original schedules.

verified Zero Surcharge Guarantee: There are never extra charges if your flight is held in holding stacks or delayed by air traffic control.

All Cityline airport pickups include our standard Executive Meet & Greet service. Your chauffeur enters the arrivals terminal directly following international customs clearance and waits in the arrivals concourse holding a clean electronic tablet showing your lead passenger surname or corporate entity name.

You will also receive an SMS 20 minutes prior to landing containing your driver's portrait, full name, direct UK mobile number, and car registration.

We provide 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time for all standard airport terminal arrivals (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, and London City), starting exactly when your aircraft wheels touch the runway.

For non-airport domestic transfers (hotel or residential pickups across Greater London), 15 minutes of complimentary buffer time is included.

Yes, completely. Every Cityline rate is 100% all-inclusive. All mandatory London municipal fees—including the TfL Central Congestion Charge (£15), Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) compliance levies, and official airport terminal drop-off/barrier entry charges—are bundled into your upfront quoted rate.

The price stated on your initial confirmation email is the exact, final amount charged. No unannounced forecourt tolls.

Vehicles are certified under strict Transport for London passenger and luggage capacity guidelines:

Executive Saloon
Up to 3 passengers • 2 large check-in suitcases (23kg) + 2 carry-ons
Estate Car
Up to 4 passengers • 4 large suitcases + 2 carry-ons
Executive MPV / V-Class
Up to 7 passengers • 7 large check-in bags + 6 cabin bags

Yes. While UK law exempts licensed private hire vehicles from child restraint obligations in emergencies, Cityline provides certified ECE R44/04 and R129 i-Size seats free of charge upon request.

Simply select either Group 0+ Infant Seat (0-13kg), Group 1 Toddler Seat (9-18kg), or Group 2/3 Booster Cushion during Step 2 of online checkout, and your vehicle will arrive pre-fitted and sanitized.

Cityline offers unconditional full refunds for standard bookings cancelled up to 12 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. Cancellations can be triggered directly using the one-click link inside your booking confirmation email or via our 24/7 UK control desk.

If an airline cancels your flight completely, simply notify our dispatch desk with your flight number and we will either rebook your vehicle at no cost or issue an immediate 100% card reversal.

A HMRC-compliant VAT receipt (including Cityline Transfers Ltd VAT registration number, journey breakdown, driver ID, and timestamp) is automatically generated and emailed to your billing address within 10 minutes of journey completion.

Corporate clients with invoiced 30-day credit facilities receive an itemised single monthly CSV/PDF ledger broken down by employee reference, cost code, and journey route.
