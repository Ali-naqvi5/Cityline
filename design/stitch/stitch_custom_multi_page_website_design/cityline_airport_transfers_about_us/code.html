Our Founding Purpose

Built on punctuality, not surge algorithms.

Cityline was founded in London with an uncompromising mandate: to eliminate the systemic friction of app-based rideshare at major airports. Travellers touchdown after 10-hour transcontinental flights only to suffer phantom driver cancellations, unexpected surge multipliers, and confusing terminal parking rendezvous.

We discarded app gamification in favor of operational precision. Every booking connects to live ADS-B flight telemetry, automatically adjusting our chauffeur dispatch times to your aircraft’s touchdown wheels-down marker. Whether your inbound flight arrives 45 minutes early or encounters severe holding patterns over Biggin Hill, your car arrives in perfect alignment.

By retaining 60 minutes of complimentary terminal waiting, including car park forecourt clearances, and staffing a permanent human dispatch office in Central London, we offer international executives and families seamless peace of mind from runway to doorstep.
