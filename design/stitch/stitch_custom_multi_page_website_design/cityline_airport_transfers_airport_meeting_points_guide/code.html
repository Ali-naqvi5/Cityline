Collect Luggage & Proceed to Customs

After collecting your luggage from carousels 1–6, follow the illuminated overhead signs through the green 'Nothing to Declare' or red customs clearance channel.

Exit into the Public Concourse

Walk through the double automatic sliding glass doors into the main public concourse. Look immediately forward toward the primary arrival greeting barrier.

Locate Your Chauffeur at Barrier

Your chauffeur will be waiting directly beside the greeting barrier holding your personalised name board. They will introduce themselves, verify your destination, and assist with your luggage to the vehicle.
