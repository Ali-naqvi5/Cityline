About this journey

Connecting between London Heathrow and London Gatwick requires crossing the southwestern quadrant of London via the M25 motorway corridor, covering approximately 45 miles. Unlike journeys into Central London, this orbital transfer avoids congestion charge zones and provides point-to-point transit directly between terminal forecourts.

For international connecting passengers with flights booked across both hubs, punctuality and zero handling stress are paramount. Cityline chauffeurs monitor both your inbound flight into Heathrow and live M25 motorway telemetry to route via optimal junctions (M4/M25/M23) ensuring comfortable arrival at Gatwick departures.

info M25 Traffic & Transfer Window Advice

We advise allowing a minimum connection window of 3.5 hours between scheduled flight touchdown at Heathrow and scheduled departure at Gatwick. During weekday peak hours (07:00–09:30 and 16:30–19:00), M25 traffic between Junction 12 (M3) and Junction 8 (Reigate) can add 20–35 minutes.
