What happens next

Here is how your transfer will unfold seamlessly on the day of travel.

1. We monitor your flight

Our dispatch team monitors flight BA 1392 via live radar. If your flight lands early or experiences a delay, your chauffeur's arrival time adjusts automatically at no extra charge.

2. Chauffeur details dispatched

12 hours before your scheduled touchdown, we will SMS and email you your assigned driver's full name, direct contact number, photo, and vehicle registration.

3. Terminal Meet & Greet

Your chauffeur will wait inside Heathrow Terminal 2 arrivals concourse holding a clear Cityline welcome board with your name, assisting you with your luggage to the vehicle.
