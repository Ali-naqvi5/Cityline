1

Automated ADS-B flight telemetry and live tracking

Unlike standard private hire applications relying on unverified passenger schedules, Cityline's dispatch core connects directly to global Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) radar tracking transponders and National Air Traffic Services (NATS) airspace telemetry.

When you enter your flight number during reservation (e.g. BA 117, VS 45, or EK 29), our automated dispatch system benchmarks the exact airframe serial. Chauffeur dispatch times are synchronized to the aircraft's physical touchdown point on the runway—not the scheduled gate arrival time.

hourglass_top Air Traffic Holding Stacks

If your inbound aircraft is placed into a London holding stack at Bovingdon, Lambourne, Biggin, or Ockham for 45 minutes, your chauffeur remains parked at the authorized staging buffer outside the terminal perimeter without accumulating fees.

bolt Early Inbound Landings

If favorable jet streams result in your aircraft touching down 30 minutes ahead of schedule, our system flags the early arrival to your driver to ensure immediate curbside staging.

info Key Passenger Takeaway

You never need to purchase onboard Wi-Fi or text our operations centre while airborne if your flight encounters headwinds or holds. Our automated dispatch system recalculates your chauffeur's terminal entry in real time based on continuous transponder pings.

2

Complimentary 60-minute airport grace period

Every airport booking with Cityline automatically includes a complimentary 60-minute complimentary waiting window. This grace period does not commence at your scheduled booking time; it activates the exact moment your aircraft's wheels make physical contact with the runway (confirmed via ADS-B touchdown confirmation).

3

Severely delayed or diverted flights

In the event of severe adverse weather, airspace ground stops, or technical diversions where an aircraft is rerouted to a secondary London airport (e.g., diverted from Gatwick LGW to Stansted STN or Birmingham BHX), our operations protocol guarantees zero penalties for passenger inconvenience:

alt_route
Airport Diverted Prior to Descent

If diverted to another London commercial airport, our dispatch team can immediately redirect a regional driver to the new airfield terminal or offer a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fees.

update
Delays Over 3 Hours

Flights delayed beyond 180 minutes are automatically reassigned to a rested relief chauffeur in accordance with UK TfL driver fatigue regulations, ensuring your executive vehicle is waiting when your delayed flight arrives.

5

How to contact your chauffeur upon landing

Our seamless dispatch workflow ensures you are connected to your driver before you step off the aircraft:

1
Pre-touchdown SMS

15 minutes before landing, you receive an automated SMS & WhatsApp containing driver name, contact phone, vehicle model, and registration number.

2
Terminal Staging

Your chauffeur enters the short-stay arrivals car park 25 minutes after touchdown and proceeds on foot to the designated terminal meeting point.

3
iPad Meet & Greet

Your driver stands in the arrivals foyer holding an electronic digital nameboard. You can message or call them directly via WhatsApp at any point.
