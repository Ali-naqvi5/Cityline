Clear Passenger Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding our London Heathrow airport transfer protocol and passenger policies.

help Where will my driver be waiting inside Heathrow Terminal 2?

Your chauffeur will be waiting inside the Terminal 2 arrivals public concourse at Marker 3, immediately past customs and the baggage exit. They will hold an official Cityline digital board displaying your lead passenger name.

flight_takeoff What happens if my inbound flight to Heathrow is delayed?

All flights are monitored in real time using live air traffic radar feeds. We automatically shift your pickup schedule to your actual touchdown time, and your 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time starts only when wheels touch the runway.

receipt_long Is the Heathrow terminal drop-off/pickup fee included in the price?

Yes. Every quote from Cityline is completely inclusive. The £5.00 Heathrow terminal barrier drop-off charge, short-stay car park pickup tariffs, Congestion Charge, and London ULEZ fees are already fully incorporated with zero surprise additions.

luggage How much luggage can I bring in each vehicle class?

Our Standard Saloons accommodate 2 check-in cases plus 2 cabin bags. Executive Mercedes-Benz saloons take 3 check-in cases. For larger parties or sports equipment, MPV 5 carries up to 5 large suitcases, and MPV 8 carries 8 full-sized flight cases.

child_friendly Can I request child safety seats for my journey from Heathrow?

Yes, rear-facing infant carriers, forward-facing toddler seats, and booster cushions can be requested during booking at no additional charge. Every device conforms strictly to UK and EU ECE R44/04 or R129 (i-Size) safety specifications.

event_busy What is the cancellation and refund policy if my flight is cancelled?

If your flight is cancelled by the airline prior to aircraft departure, you are eligible for an immediate 100% full refund or free rescheduling to your rebooked transit slot. Simply alert our 24/7 operations team as soon as your carrier notifies you.
